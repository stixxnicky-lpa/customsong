Why I write songs♪

I've been writing lyrics and poetry since my high school years. I went on to lead and perform in original bands, including Leeds based bands Indica, Indiskies and Myriad Blue, and Sheffield band Q.

The year 2000 I upped Stixx and moved to Mallorca where the next 13 years were spent performing at some of Mallorca's best venues, corporate events, wedding's and private parties.

In 2013 a problem with my voice box meant hanging up the microphone for good. Recently I came back to my love of writing, and it felt right to use those years of songwriting to make songs for other people: the ones that say what you've always wanted to say.

Privacy

Who I am. Nicky Stixx, songwriter, based in Mallorca, Spain. Contact: reply to any email from me.

What I collect. Only what you type into the questionnaire: your name and email, and the details you give me about the person the song is for.

Why. To write your song, send it to you, and handle payment and my accounting records. I don't use it for marketing and I don't sell or share it.

Who handles it. The questionnaire goes through Formspree, which emails it to me. Payment is made on PayPal, so I never see your card details. This page loads fonts from Google Fonts and videos from YouTube, which may receive your IP address when they load.

How long. For as long as I need it to deliver your song and to keep records the law requires, then I delete it.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting me. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD). Please only share details about other people that you're comfortable sharing.

Terms

What you get. One original, personalised song written from your questionnaire, delivered as an audio file by email within 14 days of receiving both your story and your payment. The price is €150, paid through PayPal.

Made to order. Because every song is written specially for you, I can't offer a refund once I've started writing. If you change your mind before I begin, tell me and I'll refund you in full.

If something's wrong. If a name, date or fact in your song is incorrect, tell me and I'll fix it.

Using your song. It's yours to enjoy and share with family and friends, and to play at your event. If you'd like to release or sell it, please ask me first. I'd love to share a short snippet as an example, and I'll always ask you before I do.
